Bicol Express Recipe

Description

A popular Filipino stew characterized by its fiery heat, rich creaminess, and deeply savory flavor profile. Originating from the Bicol region of the Philippines—an area famous for its heavy use of chili peppers and coconut milk—this dish features bite-sized pieces of pork belly simmered in a luscious sauce made from coconut milk and coconut cream. The signature punch comes from an abundance of sliced siling haba (long green chilis) and siling labuyo (bird's eye chilis), which are balanced and enhanced by the umami depth of fermented shrimp paste (bagoong).

Ingredients

  • Pork belly: 2 lbs (1 kg), cut into bite-sized cubes
  • Coconut milk: 1 cup
  • Long green chilis (siling haba): 2 to 3 cups, sliced
  • Bird's eye chilis (siling labuyo): 3 to 5, chopped (adjust for heat)
  • Shrimp paste (bagoong alamang): 3 to 4 tablespoons
  • Garlic: 5 cloves, minced
  • Onion: 1 medium, chopped
  • Onion: 1 tablespoon, minced
  • Cooking oil: 1 tablespoon
  • Salt and black pepper: To taste

Steps

  1. Prep the chilis: De-seed the long green chilis if you want to reduce the heat, then slice them into rings. Chop the bird's eye chilis.
  2. Sauté the aromatics: Heat oil in a large pan or wok over medium heat. Sauté the garlic, onions, and ginger until fragrant and translucent.
  3. Brown the pork: Add the pork belly to the pan. Cook until the meat turns light brown and starts to release its natural oils, about 5 to 7 minutes.
  4. Add shrimp paste: Stir in the shrimp paste and cook for another 2 minutes to cook out the raw flavor.
  5. Simmer in coconut milk: Pour in the coconut milk. Bring it to a boil, then lower the heat to a simmer. Cover and let it cook for 20 to 25 minutes, or until the pork becomes tender.
  6. Add the chilis and cream: Stir in the sliced green chilis, bird's eye chilis, and the thicker coconut cream.
  7. Reduce the sauce: Simmer uncovered for another 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and begins to render oil. Season with salt and pepper to taste if necessary. Serve hot with white rice.
