Tinola Recipes

Description

A classic Filipino comfort food featuring chicken simmered in a soothing, ginger-infused broth. Sautéing a generous amount of fresh ginger alongside garlic and onions creates the unmistakable warming base that makes this dish perfect for rainy days or when you need a comforting boost.

Ingredients

  • Chicken: 1 kg, cut into serving pieces
  • Cooking oil: 2 tablespoons, for sautéing
  • Ginger: 2–3 thumbs, cut into thin strips (julienned)
  • Garlic: 4 cloves, minced
  • Onion: 1 medium, chopped
  • Fish sauce (patis): 3 tablespoons, for savory depth
  • Water (or rice washing): 5–6 cups, for the broth base
  • Chayote (sayote) or green papaya: 1 medium, cut into wedges
  • Chili leaves or moringa (malunggay): 1 cup, stems removed
  • Salt and pepper: to taste, for

Steps

  1. Sauté the aromatics: Heat oil and sauté the ginger, garlic, and onions until soft and fragrant.
  2. Sear the chicken: Add chicken pieces and cook for 5 minutes until light brown on the outside.
  3. Season the meat: Pour in the fish sauce and stir for 2 minutes to coat the chicken.
  4. Simmer the broth: Pour in water, bring to a boil, cover, and simmer for 20 minutes until tender.
  5. Cook the vegetables: Add chayote or green papaya wedges and simmer for 7 minutes until fork-tender.
  6. Wilt the greens: Adjust seasoning, stir in chili or malunggay leaves, and turn off heat to let greens wilt.
